Whisper word — a word the ASR actually heard in the vocals, with its forced-alignment start/end (arrows). Drag notes so they sit under the word they sing. Load pitch JSON in the toolbar swaps in a different whisperx_pitch.json overlay.
CREPE pitch dot — one per pitch-detected audio frame (y = detected pitch). Color = loudness (green quiet → red loud); transparency = pitch confidence, so faint dots are less trustworthy.
FFT background — live spectrogram of the dropped vocals, one row per semitone: dark = quiet, bright red = energy. It appears only after loading audio.
Beat grid — one vertical line per beat (quarter note); the brighter, thicker line marks every 4th beat (the bar line the metronome accents).
